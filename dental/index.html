[ FAQ ]

Questions doctors ask.

Will it be slow on patients' phones?

No. The 3D scene is rendered live and tuned for mid-range Android phones, with a lighter version for older devices.

Do I need to provide anything?

Your Instagram, your logo if you have one, and 10 minutes on WhatsApp. We pull the rest from your public profiles and confirm everything with you before launch.

Can I change prices or offers later?

Yes. Send a message and it's updated within a day on the care plan, or per request without it.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your clinic's name and you get the full source code.

What if I don't like it?

You see a full preview before paying the second 50%. Two rounds of revisions are included.
