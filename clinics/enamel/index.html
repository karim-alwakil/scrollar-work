Step 00 / The scan

Twelve seconds. No trays.

A light wand passes over your teeth and builds a 3D model accurate to a tenth of a millimetre. No gagging moulds, no guessing.

Layer 01 / Enamel

The hardest thing in your body.

Stains, chips and wear live here. Whitening, bonding and veneers work on this outer shell, and nothing deeper.

Layer 02 / Dentin

Where cavities spread.

Softer than enamel, so decay moves fast once it gets in. Caught on the scan early, it is one white filling and twenty minutes.

Layer 03 / Pulp

The nerve. The part that hurts.

If decay reaches here, a root canal saves the tooth. At Enamel it is usually one calm visit under local anaesthetic.
