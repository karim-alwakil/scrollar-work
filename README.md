# Scrollar work

Live demos of 3D and scroll-animated websites by Scrollar (concept projects for fictional businesses).

- Enamel, dental studio: a scanned molar turns from photo to X-ray layer by layer (Three.js, custom shaders, clipping planes)
- Dar Dental: a real clinic day as a horizontal film strip with a running clock (Lenis, scroll-driven video)
- Maqass, barber atelier: cinematic footage and editorial type, frame shrink on scroll
- Dahab Air Clash: drone flight over a kitesurf lagoon (WebGL shaders)
- Qamar One: a smart-home product that explodes into its parts on scroll (Three.js)
- Lumen Dental: an implant that assembles itself as you scroll

3D scans credited on each page (CC BY 4.0). Footage: Mixkit.
