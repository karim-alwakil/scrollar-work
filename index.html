[ FAQ ]

What buyers ask before they start.

Anything else, ask on the call.

How long does a project take?

The free demo on your project takes a few days. A product configurator usually launches in 2 to 4 weeks and an off-plan sales experience in 3 to 6 weeks, depending on the number of products, units and integrations. You get a fixed timeline with the quote.

Who owns the code and the 3D models?

You do. Once the project is paid, the code, 3D models and content are yours, and we hand over the full source on request. White-label work for agencies is delivered without Scrollar branding.

Can it plug into our website, Shopify store or CRM?

Yes. It can run on its own subdomain or be embedded in your existing site. Configurators map options to Shopify or store variants and add to the cart. Leads from unit sheets and saved designs can be sent to your CRM, a webhook, a Google Sheet or email.

Where is it hosted, and what does the monthly fee cover?

We host it on a fast global CDN. The monthly fee covers hosting, updates to availability and prices, browser and device fixes, and small changes. If you prefer to host it yourselves, we hand everything over.

Do you support Arabic and right-to-left layouts?

Yes. Every build can ship in Arabic and English with a proper right-to-left layout, Arabic typography and translated unit sheets and labels, not just mirrored text.

Will the 3D work on phones and slow connections?

Yes. Models are optimised for the web (see GLB Diet), load progressively and fall back to a lighter version on older phones. The tool stays usable on mobile data.

What do you need from us for the free demo?

For real estate: floor plans, a unit list or price sheet, and any renders. For products: product photos or CAD files and the list of options. Rough material is fine.
