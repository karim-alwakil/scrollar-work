[ FAQ ]

Questions owners ask us.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person answers.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you get the full code. You can move it anywhere, any time.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, security certificate, small text and price edits, the booking system and a monthly report. Cancel with 30 days notice and we hand everything over.

How does the free preview work?

Send us your Instagram and services. Within 24 hours you get a link to a first version built for your business. You pay nothing unless you go ahead.

Will the 3D work on older phones?

Yes. Older phones and slow connections get a lighter version of the scene automatically, and booking always works.

How do bookings reach me?

As a WhatsApp message with the service, time and client name. Booking sites also get an owner page with the day's schedule.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds of revisions before launch are included. After launch, small text and price edits are part of the monthly plan.

How do I pay?

InstaPay or bank transfer: 50% to start and 50% at launch. Gulf and Europe clients pay by international bank transfer.

Is everything in Arabic and English?

Yes. Every site comes in both, with proper right-to-left layout and Egyptian or Gulf Arabic copy.
